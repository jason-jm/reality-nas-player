[中文产品介绍](../../README.md) · [English product guide](../../README.en.md)

这些是 **Reality NAS Player v1.0 的产品界面**。头显画面来自 PICO 模拟器，桌面画面来自桌面版自身的界面。为了在 GitHub 上看清楚，有些图片把全景实拍与同一功能的局部特写并排展示。图中的片库只用于演示，播放器不附送电影。

The headset images were captured in a PICO emulator. The desktop images show the app’s own Compose UI with the same fictional library. Some plates pair a full view with a crop for legibility. The UI was captured in Chinese and English; it was not drawn by an image model.

<summary>演示画面的创作提示词 / Original film-art prompts</summary>

Only the demo films’ artwork was created with the built-in image generator. The prompts below describe the film frames, not the app or any product controls.

**Polar Letter**

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a single original, text-free, breathtaking cinematic key-art plate for a fictional independent short film about glacial light, intended to play as demo media in a real home-cinema app. A glowing minuscule amber survey shelter at the edge of a broad blue-black snow plain, colossal striated curved glacial cliffs naturally framing a portal of icy periwinkle sky and a soft lavender aurora. A single bright warm line low on the horizon. Strong readable composition in landscape 3:2; intimate textured analogue 65mm still photography, beautifully restrained lighting and real grain, deep cobalt, pale luminous ice, tiny warm amber. Carve striking, simple sculptural shapes with light. Keep the visual focal point gently right of centre but the image should also hold as a vertical crop. This is film artwork, not an illustration of the application or a rendering of a feature. Edge-to-edge, NO letters, text, logos, borders, UI, people, commercial film references, fake hardware or watermarks.

**Salt Route**

Use case: ads-marketing. One premium original cinematic still, completely text-free, for the fictional short film in a self-hosted cinema app's demonstration library. Landscape 3:2, also strong when cropped 2:3 in the middle. A long sinuous ink-black painted road and a single tiny old ivory station wagon cut through terracotta salt flats. Straight overhead aerial, enormous negative space, warm coral / coral pink / burnt sienna with one bright cream line. The roadway enters top right, curves near the centre, exits bottom left: a beautiful, instantly legible hand drawn S at thumbnail size. Art-directed real medium-format photography on coarse 35mm stock, spare and graphic, fine mineral texture, no lettering at all, no other vehicles, no credits, no logos, no frames, no UI or recognisable properties.

**Tide Garden**

Use case: ads-marketing. Make one original key-art photograph for a fictional nature documentary in the demo catalogue of an independent cinema player. Landscape 3:2 image, striking in a central 2:3 crop. In the calm jade-green ocean beneath a rippled glass sea surface, a single long ribbon of kelp reaches up, its layered blades fanning like botanical cyanotype against a luminous shaft of warm mint light from the top right. Elegant film-grain texture, photographic and a little uncanny, boldly graphic tactile shapes, deep petroleum teal at edges and a pale celadon centre, no coral reef, no fish swarm, no diver, no text, borders, credits, logos or UI. Authentic underwater art photography, not vector illustration, quiet drama.

**Lakeside panorama**

Use case: ads-marketing. A single original 360-degree equirectangular still made as demonstration media for a real panoramic video player. Full spherical landscape, ultra-wide 2:1 equirectangular map, perfectly level horizon halfway up the image. Camera is on the shore of a quiet lake at twilight, low jagged alpine ridgeline rings the entire horizon, deep blue mist, a sliver of soft coral evening light and its reflection across the water straight ahead in the central portion. Conifers on the banks on either side; stars only just beginning in the cool periwinkle sky. Zenith open gradient sky and nadir pebbles at shoreline, surroundings meet gently at left/right longitude seam, calm HDRI-inspired environmental photography. No text, UI, map grids, logos, people, resorts or recognisable properties. Readable broad forms and a natural painterly photographic palette; it should look beautiful as a projected real headset viewport.
