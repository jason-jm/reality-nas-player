# Inside the frames

[Product guide](../../README.md) · [Choose a language](../languages/README.md)

The poster wall uses a 4:3 page so you can see the whole library window. The other posters use a 3:2 page. Each screenshot keeps its original proportions. We calibrated the headset capture with a square and a circle before taking the pictures. In the 1920 × 1920 capture the square measured 149 × 148 pixels and the circle 150 × 149.

The app scanned eight film folders on a demonstration WebDAV share with online lookup switched off. For *Polar Letter*, it found `Polar.Letter.2026.720p.mp4`, a Kodi `movie.nfo` with a sample ID, `poster.jpg` and `fanart.jpg`. The title, year, synopsis and credits came from the NFO. The covers came from their respective folders. The first row in the scanning poster and the full library in the next poster come from one capture of that result.

The covers, synopses, subtitle line and sample footage belong to a fictional programme made for these pictures. The artwork is original and the demonstration films are not included with the player. The library, rooms, playback controls and settings are app captures. See the [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the environment and Earth imagery credits.
