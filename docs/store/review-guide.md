# Reality NAS Player 1.1.0 (6)

## 中文审核说明

本应用播放用户带入的影片。审核附件中的 `Reality-NAS-Player-Review.mp4` 是一段 45 秒的动态测试图，带有轻声测试音及中、英两条内嵌字幕。以下步骤可在头显或 PICO 模拟器上完成，全程无需账号或 NAS。

1. 解压附件，将 MP4 复制到头显内部共享存储的 `Movies` 文件夹。用系统文件管理器打开影片，在应用选择器中选择 **Reality NAS Player**。
2. 如需验证片库入口，打开应用，进入「来源 → 本地媒体 → 扫描本地」，根据系统提示允许读取视频。在「全部」中打开测试片。希望保留测试片原文件名时，可在扫描前进入「设置 → 刮削与元数据」，开启「离线刮削」。
3. 播放后确认彩色图形在移动，暂停后图形静止。拖至 00:15，打开「字幕轨道」，分别选择中文、英文和「关闭」，观察同一段画面的字幕变化。
4. 在播放控件的「观影空间」中选择「虚拟环境 → 太空观景厅」。进入后打开「座位」，在「中央」与「右侧靠窗」之间切换，观察窗框、地球和银幕的相对位置。

5. 验证免密钥电影匹配时，保留原测试片，另外复制一份并命名为 `Interstellar.2014.mp4`。在默认在线设置下扫描该目录。片库应出现 2014 年的《星际穿越》海报，播放内容仍是测试图。

## English review instructions

Reality NAS Player plays films supplied by the viewer. The included `Reality-NAS-Player-Review.mp4` is a 45-second moving test pattern with a low-volume tone and embedded English and Chinese subtitles. Both a headset and the PICO emulator support this walkthrough. No account or NAS is required.

1. Unzip the attachment and copy the MP4 into the `Movies` folder in shared headset storage. Open it in the system file manager and choose **Reality NAS Player** in the app picker.
2. To test library import, open **Sources → On this headset → Scan this headset**. Grant video access when prompted, then open the clip under **All**. To keep the filename as its title, enable **Settings → Scraping → Offline scraping** before scanning.
3. Play and pause to check that the coloured shapes move and stop. Seek to 00:15. In **Subtitles**, select Chinese, English, and **Off** in turn and check that the text changes.
4. In the playback controls, choose **Viewing space → Places → Space lounge**. Open **Seat** and switch between **Center** and **Right window**. The window frames, Earth and the cinema screen should shift relative to the viewer.

5. To check movie matching, make a second copy of the supplied test pattern named `Interstellar.2014.mp4`. Scan that folder with the default online settings. The library should show the 2014 film and its poster. Playing the copy still shows the supplied test pattern.
